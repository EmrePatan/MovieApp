Movie Cave is a mobile app for discovering titles, keeping watchlists, tracking what you have watched, rating and reviewing, and following TV shows for release updates.

Discover movies & TV

Search and browse catalog content sourced through licensed movie and TV metadata providers, including details, cast, credits, collections, and where-to-watch information.

Watchlists & history

Save titles to watchlists, mark movies and TV episodes as watched, and review your progress over time.

Ratings & reviews

Rate titles and write reviews that are stored with your account so you can revisit your opinions later.

Follow TV shows

Follow TV series to receive in-app notifications about relevant new seasons or episodes when you enable notifications on your device.

Personalized recommendations

Optional AI-assisted movie recommendations use your in-app activity to suggest titles. See our Privacy Policy for details on what is sent to the AI provider.

Account & control

Sign in with email or supported social providers. You can delete your account from inside the app. See Delete account for the exact steps. For support, email support@moviecaveapp.com.
