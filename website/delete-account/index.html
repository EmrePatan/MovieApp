Delete your account

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Movie Cave is the mobile app and related services described on this site. You can delete specific account-associated data without deleting your account, or permanently delete your entire Movie Cave account from inside the app. This page describes both flows and what happens to your data.

Delete specific data without deleting your account

If you want to remove particular content stored with your Movie Cave account but keep your account, use the in-app actions below while signed in. These actions remove the selected data from our primary application database. They do not delete your account credentials, profile, or unrelated activity unless you choose the full account deletion flow described later on this page.

Favorites

  • Open a favorited movie or TV show from anywhere in the app, then on the detail screen tap Favorite again to remove it from your favorites.

Watchlists and watchlist items

  • On a movie or TV detail screen, tap Watchlist, then tap a list that already contains the title to remove it from that list.
  • Go to the Library tab, open the Watchlists category, open a list, then long-press a poster to remove that title from the list.
  • To delete an entire watchlist, open the list from Library → Watchlists, tap the menu (⋯), choose Delete List, and confirm.

Ratings

  • On a movie or TV detail screen, find the Your Rating section. Clear your rating by dragging the star control to the far-left clear zone, or by using the accessibility decrease control until the rating is cleared.

Reviews

  • On a movie or TV detail screen, open Reviews, tap Edit on your review, then tap Delete review and confirm.

Watch history

  • On a movie detail screen, tap Watched while the title is marked watched to mark it as unwatched and remove it from your watch history.
  • On a TV show detail screen, tap Watched while the show is marked watched to remove the show-level watched state from your history.
  • On a TV show detail screen, open Seasons and toggle a season off to remove that season's watched state from your history.
  • On a season page, toggle an episode off to remove that episode from your watch history.

Search history

  • Open Search from Home or Discover. In Recent Searches, tap the delete control on an individual query to remove that entry.
  • On the same screen, tap Clear all to remove all stored search history for your account.

Notifications

  • From Home, tap the notifications bell, then swipe a notification left and choose delete, or use the accessibility delete action on a notification row.
  • Read in-app notifications that remain in your inbox may be removed automatically after a configured retention period (currently seven days for read notifications), as described in our Privacy Policy.

Catalog follows (release alerts)

  • For an unreleased movie with release alerts enabled, open the movie detail screen and tap the release alert action again to turn the alert off and remove the follow record.
  • For a TV show, open the detail screen, tap Follow or Manage follow, turn off both new-season and new-episode notification preferences, then save to unfollow the show.

Push notification device registration

  • Movie Cave does not provide a separate "unregister this device" button in the app. When you sign out from Profile, the app removes locally stored push registration state and unregisters the known push device with our servers for that session.
  • You can also disable Movie Cave notifications in your device operating system settings.

What may remain after deleting specific data

  • Deleting individual items removes them from our primary application database, but unrelated account data and other categories listed above remain unless you delete them separately.
  • Server logs and infrastructure backups may retain limited technical records for security, troubleshooting, and disaster recovery for some period determined by hosting and logging practices.
  • Aggregated product metrics are not linked to your account in our metrics store.
  • Temporary AI recommendation session cache in Redis expires automatically and is not explicitly purged when you delete individual activity items in the current implementation.

If you cannot delete specific data in the app

Email support@moviecaveapp.com from the address associated with your Movie Cave account and describe the specific data you want removed (for example, a rating, review, watchlist, or search history). Include enough information for us to identify the correct account, such as your account email address or username.

For your security, do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or other secrets in email.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Movie Cave app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. In the Account section, tap Delete account.
  4. Read the warning, then tap Continue.
  5. Confirm deletion:
    • If your account uses a password, enter your current password and tap Delete my account.
    • If your account is social-only, tap Confirm with Google or Confirm with Apple and complete the provider sign-in prompt.

After successful deletion, you are signed out and your account data is removed from our primary application database.

What we delete

When you complete account deletion, we delete your user account and associated personal data, including:

  • Account credentials and profile fields (email, username, display name, password hash, verification state)
  • Linked Google or Apple sign-in records
  • Ratings and reviews
  • Favorites and watchlists
  • Movie and TV episode watch history
  • Search history stored with your account
  • TV show and movie follow records and related notification preferences
  • In-app release notifications tied to your account
  • Registered push notification devices for your account
  • Password reset and email verification tokens associated with your account

What may remain

  • Shared catalog content such as movies, TV shows, episodes, and genres is not deleted because it is not personal data and is shared across users.
  • Aggregated product metrics (for example total screen-open counters) are not linked to your account in our metrics store.
  • Server logs and infrastructure backups may retain limited technical records (such as request timestamps, correlation identifiers, or authenticated user identifiers in logs) for security, troubleshooting, and disaster recovery for some period determined by hosting and logging practices.
  • Temporary AI recommendation session cache in Redis expires automatically and is not explicitly purged at deletion time in the current implementation.

Authentication and device data after deletion

  • Your access token becomes invalid after deletion when sessions are validated against the removed account.
  • The app removes locally stored push registration state during logout after deletion.
  • Push device registrations stored on our servers for your account are deleted with your account.

If you cannot use the in-app deletion flow

The primary way to delete your account is the in-app flow above. Movie Cave does not provide an automated web-based account deletion form.

If you cannot access the app—for example, because you uninstalled it, cannot sign in, or no longer have the device—email support@moviecaveapp.com from the address associated with your Movie Cave account and request account deletion assistance.

If you can reinstall the app, sign in, and complete the steps above, that is usually the fastest option. For password-based accounts, you can also use password reset before signing in again.
